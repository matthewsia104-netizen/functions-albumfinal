How we modelled each photo

  1. Step 1Set the origin.

    Import the photo into Desmos and place the origin and axis scale on a sensible point of the real object.

  2. Step 2Define the axes.

    Label axes and units so every grid square means something physical.

  3. Step 3Use the points.

    Pick two key points on the photo, substitute them, and solve for a, k, d, c by hand before touching a slider.

  4. Step 4Refine and restrict.

    Fine-tune in Desmos, then restrict domain and range to what physically exists in the photo.
